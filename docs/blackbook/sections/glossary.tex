\chapter*{Glossary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Glossary}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{3.9cm}p{10.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Academic scope & The set of subjects (or, in design, class offerings) within
which an actor's permissions apply. Teachers and students are restricted to the
subjects in their assignments/placements; the institute admin holds
whole-institute scope. \\
Attempt & A student's timed response to an assessment; it snapshots the chosen
questions at its start and is evaluated server-side. \\
Blueprint & A paper pattern used as a template for an assessment or question
paper. \\
Chunk & A contiguous page range of a source material (default 10 pages) that one
OCR worker claims, processes, and submits. \\
Coordinate & An OCR chunk plan, the sequence in which claims are granted, the
lease of a claimed chunk, and the aggregation of chunk results. \\
Double-submit CSRF & A CSRF defense in which the client sends the value of a
cookie in a custom header and the server requires them to match. \\
Institute & The primary tenant: a college or school that owns users,
memberships, content, and assessments. \\
Membership & The relation that makes a user a member of an institute and carries
the user's roles within it. \\
Modular monolith & A single deployable service organized into clear module
boundaries. \\
Multi-tenancy & Serving multiple independent institutes from one shared
deployment while isolating their data and permissions. \\
OmniRoute & The internal OpenAI-compatible AI gateway through which all model
calls from workers must pass. \\
Paper pattern & A declarative blueprint of sections, question types, counts,
marks, and difficulty distributions. \\
Permission & The atomic authorization unit of the form \texttt{resource.action},
e.g. \texttt{questions.update}. \\
Provisioning & Creation of users and memberships under institute control rather
than public self-registration. \\
Scope chain & The subject$\rightarrow$chapter$\rightarrow$topic hierarchy that
confines a resource to its subject. \\
Tenant & A customer organization of the platform (an institute). \\
Worker & A long-running process that consumes messages from a queue (or the OCR
coordinator) and performs heavy, asynchronous work. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{center}

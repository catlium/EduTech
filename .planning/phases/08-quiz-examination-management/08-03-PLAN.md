<objective>
Implement the assessment lifecycle: the server-side state machine
(DRAFT → PUBLISHED → ACTIVE → COMPLETED with PUBLISHED → DRAFT unpublish), the
publish validation gate that enforces EXAM-08 (only APPROVED questions) plus
non-empty, duration, max marks, and schedule preconditions, and the
complete/activate transitions. This closes EXAM-05 (publish + complete),
EXAM-06 (lifecycle), EXAM-07 (server-enforced transitions), and EXAM-08 (only
APPROVED questions in official assessments).

Purpose: The state machine is the phase's critical design element — every
transition has preconditions the backend must enforce server-side (research
Patterns 1 & 3). With no cron/auto-activation for MVP, ACTIVE is reached via a
manual activate endpoint and the schedule is checked on reads (research A1).
Output: Lifecycle-complete assessments with enforced transitions and the
published-only-APPROVED gate, verified E2E.
</objective>
